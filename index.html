The Friday-morning crisis

When Elena learned that the company’s largest supplier could not deliver, her team had to scramble to protect Monday’s production run.

Everyone was already frazzled. A system launch had consumed their attention, and nobody had managed to get the routine work off their plate.

During the crisis meeting, David began to belittle a young planner who raised a quality concern. Because there was a stigma attached to admitting uncertainty, the room fell silent.

Elena intervened: “Question the evidence, not her competence.” Without humiliating David, she firmly put him in his place.

The team found an alternative supplier. They picked themselves up, learned from the disruption, and redesigned their contingency process.
